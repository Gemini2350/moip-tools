AV sync reference signal

A test video for measuring lip sync: once per second a flash and an audio chirp occur at exactly the same moment. A QR code numbers every flash, so picture and sound can be matched even when they are seconds apart.

Play it from a file, a playout server or a streaming platform and compare picture and sound at the far end. Made for the AV Sync Tool iPhone app, which reads the offset in milliseconds (coming soon to the App Store).

All files are 1080p, 5:20 min, and were measured after conversion: picture and sound are in sync in every one. Pick the frame rate of your system.
